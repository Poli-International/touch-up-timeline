3 Tattoo Maintenance Schedule

Your personalized care routine to maximize tattoo longevity

☀️

Daily Care

  • Moisturize with fragrance-free lotion (morning & night)
  • Apply SPF 50+ sunscreen when exposed to sun
  • Keep tattoo clean with gentle soap
  • Stay hydrated (helps skin health)
📆

Weekly Care

  • Deep moisturizing treatment
  • Inspect for changes or fading
  • Check for skin irritation
  • Exfoliate gently around (not on) tattoo
📅

Monthly Care

  • Take photo to track changes over time
  • Detailed inspection for fading
  • Assess if moisturizing routine is working
  • Check for any line spreading or blurring
🗓️

Annual Care

  • Professional assessment by tattoo artist
  • Evaluate if touch-up needed
  • Compare to previous years' photos
  • Plan and budget for touch-ups if needed
