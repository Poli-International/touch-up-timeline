## What Is the Touch-up Timeline Planner?

The Touch-up Timeline Planner is a free web tool that predicts when a tattoo will need a touch-up based on seven input factors: tattoo age, style, colors used, body location, sun exposure, and care level. The tool uses a multi-factor calculation model that multiplies a base longevity value (determined by style) with modifiers for colors, placement, sun exposure, and care habits.

The calculation follows this formula: `longevity = baseLongevity × styleModifier × colorModifier × placementModifier × careModifier`

The tool outputs a personalized timeline showing years until touch-up, fading progress percentage, urgency level, color fading order, and actionable recommendations. It also includes four additional tabs: Fading Predictor (visual timeline), Maintenance Plan (care schedule), Lifetime Costs (30-year cost projection), and Touch-Up Readiness (current condition assessment).

## How to Use

### Step 1: Enter Tattoo Age
Input how many years since you got the tattoo (0 to 50 years, supports half-year increments).

### Step 2: Select Tattoo Style
Choose from eight styles: Black & Grey, Traditional/American Traditional, Color Realism, Black & Grey Realism, Watercolor, Fine Line/Single Needle, Tribal/Solid Blackwork, or Script/Lettering.

### Step 3: Choose Colors Used
Select all colors present in the tattoo from nine options: Black, Dark Blue, Purple, Red, Green, Yellow, Orange, Pink/Light Colors, and White.

### Step 4: Select Body Location
Choose from four location categories: Covered Areas (torso, upper arms, thighs), Occasionally Exposed (forearms, lower legs), Frequently Exposed (hands, face, neck), or High Friction (feet, hands, fingers).

### Step 5: Set Sun Exposure Level
Select Low (usually covered), Medium (sometimes exposed), or High (frequently in sun).

### Step 6: Indicate Care Level
Choose Excellent (daily moisturizer, always SPF), Good (regular moisturizer, SPF when exposed), Fair (occasional moisturizer, some SPF), or Poor (minimal care, no SPF).

### Step 7: View Results
Click "Calculate Touch-Up Timeline" to see your personalized results including urgency level, years until touch-up, fading progress, color fading order, and recommendations.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: How does the tool calculate my touch-up timeline?**
The algorithm multiplies a base longevity value for your tattoo style with modifiers for colors, body location, sun exposure, and care level. Each factor adjusts the expected lifespan up or down based on real-world tattoo aging data.

**Q2: What does "fading progress" mean?**
Fading progress shows what percentage of your tattoo's expected lifespan has passed. For example, 40% fading progress means you have used 40% of the time before a touch-up is typically needed.

**Q3: Why do light colors fade faster?**
White, yellow, and pink pigments have smaller pigment particles that break down faster under UV exposure and skin regeneration. Black pigment has the largest particles and is most stable.

**Q4: Can I use this tool for a tattoo that is already overdue for touch-up?**
Yes. The tool will show "Overdue" urgency and recommend contacting an artist immediately. It still calculates the remaining timeline and provides care recommendations.

**Q5: Does the tool account for different artists' skill levels?**
No. The tool assumes standard professional application. Artist skill, needle depth, and ink quality can significantly affect actual longevity beyond what this tool predicts.

**Q6: How accurate is the cost calculator?**
The cost calculator uses a 50% of original price estimate for touch-ups, which is the industry average. Actual costs vary by artist, location, and tattoo complexity.

**Q7: What is the "Touch-Up Readiness" tab for?**
It assesses your tattoo's current condition based on fading level, color loss, line blurring, and detail loss. It provides a score and recommends whether to touch up now, monitor, or wait.

**Q8: Can I embed this tool on my studio's website?**
Yes. Click the "Free Embed" button in the header to get an iframe code. Three sizes are available: compact (600px), standard (800px), and large (1000px).

**Q9: Does sun exposure really make tattoos fade 40-60% faster?**
Yes. The tool's data shows high sun exposure can accelerate fading by 40-60%. UV rays break down ink pigments, especially in lighter colors. SPF 50+ is recommended.

**Q10: Why does the tool ask about care level?**
Care level is one of the most controllable factors. Excellent care (daily moisturizer, consistent SPF) can extend tattoo life by 30-50% compared to poor care.
